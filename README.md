Heritage Treasures: An In-Depth Analysis of UNESCO World Heritage Sites (2019)

An exploratory data analysis project examining the 2019 dataset of UNESCO World Heritage Sites — uncovering patterns in geographic distribution, site categories, inscription trends over time, and regional representation across the world's most culturally and naturally significant locations.

📌 Overview

UNESCO World Heritage Sites represent locations of outstanding cultural, historical, scientific, or natural significance, recognized and protected under international law. This project analyzes the official 2019 UNESCO dataset to answer questions such as:

How are heritage sites distributed across countries and continents?
What proportion of sites are Cultural, Natural, or Mixed?
Which years and decades saw the most inscriptions?
Are certain regions underrepresented on the World Heritage List?
What trends emerge in site categories over time?
📂 Dataset
Source: UNESCO World Heritage List (2019 edition)
Format: CSV
Key fields: Site name, country, region, category (Cultural / Natural / Mixed), year inscribed, coordinates, description
